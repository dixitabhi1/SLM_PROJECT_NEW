5. Factual Findings Derived Directly from the Data

  • Parity on Specialized Atomic Tasks: On both Atomic SQL (11/15, 73.3%) and Atomic Maths (13/15, 86.7%), the 3.82B SLM pipeline achieves exact empirical parity with GPT-OSS-120B B1 (73.3% and 86.7%) and qwen/qwen3.8-27b B1 (73.3% and 86.7%). Parametric scaling provides zero accuracy advantage over specialized tool execution on these domains.
  • Dominance in Tool-Required Composition: On Compound Tool-Required tasks, the SLM pipeline scores 85.0% (17/20), beating GPT-OSS-120B B1 (80.0%, 16/20) and qwen/qwen3.8-27b B1 (70.0%, 14/20). In head-to-head item comparisons, 3 of the 5 SLM wins against 120B occur on tool-required compound problems.
  • The "Reasoning Gap" Lies in Pure In-Context Composition: The primary performance deficit between the 3.82B SLM and the 120B LLM is localized almost entirely within Compound Solvable without Tools (15/20 for SLM vs 20/20 for 120B B1). Of the 14 items where 120B beat the SLM, 5 are pure symbolic compound problems requiring extensive multi-step in-context state tracking.
  • Collapsing Baselines Without Tools: When frontier LLMs are deprived of tools (B0), their performance collapses on composite tasks (120B B0 achieved 0/20 on tool-required items, and 27B B0 achieved 2/20). Across the total Track A split, Specialist SLM v1.3 outperforms both 120B B0 (79.0% vs 73.0%) and 27B B0 (79.0% vs 75.0%).
  • Complete Ceiling Saturation on Track B: On the synthetic HyperGrid retrieval corpus, all tool-equipped models (SLM v1.0, SLM v1.3, 27B B1, 120B B1) achieved 50/50 (100.0%) pass rates. The BM25 top-5 retrieval harness combined with strict ≤ 75-word key-value generation completely saturates the Track B benchmark.

6. Methodological Caveats & Threats to Validity

  • Secondary Held-Out Status: Because the held-out split was inspected during Phase 4 debugging and failure diagnosis, these results must be interpreted as secondary. The final definitive trial requires execution on the locked, untouched 100-item reserve split.
  • Asymmetric Baseline Tool Routing Advantage: Baselines B0 and B1 were provided ground-truth task category tags at runtime, granting them optimal tool selection without routing overhead. In contrast, Specialist SLM v1.3 is purely autonomous, inferring tool needs and routing paths dynamically from prompt text alone.
  • Floating-Point Non-Determinism Across Concurrency Slots: In local GPU execution (Vulkan0 with --np 3 parallel slots), non-atomic reduction operations in llama.cpp introduce minor sampling variance (~1 in 20 items) even at temperature = 0.0.
  • Track B Benchmark Ceiling Effect: Because Track B evaluates factual extraction from a 50-item synthetic corpus, all systems achieved 100.0%. A more challenging multi-hop reasoning corpus is required to differentiate retrieval synthesis capabilities.
  • Active Latency and Cost Invariance: While the SLM pipeline executes locally with zero monetary cost, end-to-end multi-step tool execution took on average 8–18 seconds per compound item, which was not directly latency-matched against high-throughput cloud API streaming.
